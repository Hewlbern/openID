FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about your handle and Solid passport.

What does the AI passport actually do?

It gives you a Solid WebID, a personal pod, and machine-readable access control so AI apps can use your context only with permission.

What can apps actually see?

Only what your ACL grants. Public read is limited to the profile and anything you put in /public. Writes need a token, DPoP proof, or an agent signature.

Where does my data live?

On this Solid server, in your pod. Audit events can also be pinned to IPFS and anchored on Bitcoin calendars via OpenTimestamps.

Is this another social network?

No. You can publish a page, but the product is a pod you control — not a feed you owe anyone.

What does it cost?

This community server is free to try. You can also run the Go binary yourself and keep the keys.

Can my agent or company use this?

Yes. Register an agent, issue client credentials, or sign requests with Ed25519. The API is Solid LDP plus a thin identity layer.

Where does the AI run?

It doesn’t. OpenID is the identity and storage layer. Your agents and apps run wherever you already run them.
